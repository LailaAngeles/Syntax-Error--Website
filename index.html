> HOW SYNTAXERROR WORKS

One mobile game, one database and one faculty portal, connected in real time.

  1. 01

    Students play

    Students solve short C# coding tasks level by level on their phone. An incorrect submission gets an instant message, like Missing quotation marks!

  3. 02

    Every attempt is saved

    Each attempt is stored in Firebase as it happens: time spent on every task, incorrect submissions, error messages and finished levels.

  5. 03

    Teachers review live

    The portal turns those records into clear charts and tables for each section, and updates by itself while students play. Every number explains how it was computed.

> INSIDE THE SYSTEM

What students get in the game and what teachers get in the portal.

FOR STUDENTS

SyntaxError mobile game

  • 19 levels, step by stepStarts with data types, printing text, user input, comments and arithmetic, then moves to more advanced C#.
  • Instant feedbackEvery incorrect submission shows a message that explains what to fix.
  • Teacher-unlocked levelsAfter finishing a level, a 6-digit code from the teacher opens the next one.
  • Practice anywhereRuns on Android phones, so practice is not limited to lab hours.
FOR TEACHERS

Data Analysis Portal

  • Live class overviewLevel completion, time recorded and incorrect submissions for each section, updated as students play.
  • Time and errors per topicSee which topics take longest, and which tasks get the most incorrect submissions.
  • Student recordsEach student's latest attempt beside their full history, with tasks and error messages, plus CSV reports.
  • Secure, section-based accessAccounts are created by the Super Admin, sign-in uses an email PIN, an account stays on one device at a time, and teachers only see their own sections.

MOBILE APP / WALKTHROUGH
STEP 01 — 04

Sign in to the game

Open SyntaxError, tap LOGIN and sign in with your school account.

  1. Email: your school email, e.g. surnamestudentnum@caloocan.sti.edu
  2. Password: your birthdate in YYYYMMDD format, e.g. 20080115
  3. Terms and Conditions: on your first sign-in, read them and tap Accept to continue
  4. Default password: when asked, choose Yes to set a new password, or No to keep it for now
Your account stays signed in on one device at a time. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password?
MOBILE APP / WALKTHROUGH
STEP 02 — 04

Use the Main Menu

After signing in, you arrive at the Main Menu.

  1. Play: opens the Level Menu
  2. Settings: adjust the game audio or log out
  3. Credits: meet the team behind the game
  4. Exit: closes the app
Log out from Settings before lending your phone to someone else.
MOBILE APP / WALKTHROUGH
STEP 03 — 04

Unlock levels in the Level Menu

The Level Menu holds 19 C# levels. Level 1 is open; a padlock marks locked levels.

  1. Complete the current level
  2. Ask your teacher for the PIN of the next level
  3. Enter the PIN to unlock it (valid for 5 minutes)
  4. Track your time, attempts and progress bar; tap Replay to play again
Tap any level to see its topic, best time, attempts, mistakes and what unlocks it.
MOBILE APP / WALKTHROUGH
STEP 04 — 04

Play Level 1

You are now in the game. Follow the task and solve each coding challenge.

  1. Task: the top-left panel tells you what to do
  2. Joystick: move around and follow the arrow to each terminal
  3. Time: tracks your timer and the tasks you have finished
  4. Codie and Pause: tap Codie for help, or Pause to stop the game
Your time and incorrect answers are recorded and shown to your teacher.

STEP 01 — 06

Sign in to the faculty portal

Select Sign In and use the school email and default password given by the Super Admin, then enter the 6-digit PIN sent to your email. On your first sign-in you accept the Terms and Privacy Notice and can change your default password. You then arrive on your Home page.

There is no public sign-up. An account stays on one device: signing in on another signs this one out.

STEP 02 — 06

Read the Class Overview

The Class Overview summarises the game records of your sections: how many students started playing, levels completed, total time recorded and incorrect submissions. Who can play each level shows which students are locked, waiting for your code, playing or finished.

Select the info icon beside any number to see how it is computed.

Open a student record

STEP 05 — 06

STEP 06 — 06

Archive and sign out

Archived students can be restored from the Archive page within seven days. When you are done, select Log Out, especially on a shared computer.
