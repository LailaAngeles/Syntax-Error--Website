> HOW SYNTAXERROR WORKS

One mobile game, one database and one faculty portal, connected in real time.

  1. 01

    Students play

    Students solve short C# coding tasks level by level on their phone. An incorrect submission gets an instant message, like Missing quotation marks!

  3. 02

    Every attempt is saved

    Each attempt is stored in Firebase as it happens: time spent on every task, incorrect submissions, error messages and finished levels.

  5. 03

    Teachers review live

    The portal turns those records into clear charts and tables for each section, and updates by itself while students play. Every number explains how it was computed.

> INSIDE THE SYSTEM

What students get in the game and what teachers get in the portal.

FOR STUDENTS

SyntaxError mobile game

  • 19 levels, step by stepStarts with data types, printing text, user input, comments and arithmetic, then moves to more advanced C#.
  • Instant feedbackEvery incorrect submission shows a message that explains what to fix.
  • Teacher-unlocked levelsAfter finishing a level, a 6-digit code from the teacher opens the next one.
  • Practice anywhereRuns on Android phones, so practice is not limited to lab hours.
FOR TEACHERS

Data Analysis Portal

  • Live class overviewLevel completion, time recorded and incorrect submissions for each section, updated as students play.
  • Time and errors per topicSee which topics take longest, which tasks get the most incorrect submissions, and the most common error messages.
  • Student recordsEach student's latest attempt beside their full history, with tasks and error messages, plus CSV reports.
  • Secure, section-based accessAccounts are created by the Super Admin, sign-in uses an email PIN, an account stays on one device at a time, and teachers only see their own sections.

Sign in with your school account

Access the mobile game using your official school account. Your default password is your birthdate in YYYYMMDD format (e.g., 20080115 for January 15, 2008).

STEP 03 — 06

Update Your Default Password

After signing in, a popup will ask whether you want to change your default password. Select Yes to open the Change Password form, where you can create and save a new password. Select No to continue using your current password.

For better account security, changing your default password is recommended.

Explore the Main Menu and Settings

From the Main Menu, select Play to access the Level Menu and begin your programming challenges. You can also open Settings to adjust the game's audio or log out of your account.

STEP 05 — 06

Select a Level and Track Your Progress

Begin your C# programming journey by playing Level 1. Once you successfully complete the level, your teacher will provide a unique PIN code to unlock the next level. Enter the correct PIN code to gain access and continue your learning journey. Each succeeding level requires a teacher-provided PIN code. Your completion times and overall game progress are displayed to help you track your achievements.

Complete Level 1, then enter the PIN code provided by your teacher to unlock the next level. Each PIN code is valid for 5 minutes. Completed levels display your recorded time, while the progress bar shows your overall game completion.

Follow the task instructions, use the joystick to explore, and ask Codie for help whenever you need guidance.

STEP 01 — 06

Sign in to the faculty portal

Select Sign In and use the school email and default password given by the Super Admin, then enter the 6-digit PIN sent to your email. On your first sign-in you accept the Terms and Privacy Notice and can change your default password. You then arrive on your Home page.

There is no public sign-up. An account stays on one device: signing in on another signs this one out.

Read the Class Overview
